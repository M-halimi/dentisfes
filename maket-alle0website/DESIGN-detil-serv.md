## Brand & Style

### Vision & Tone
The design system reflects clinical mastery, bespoke prosthodontic craft, and serene Moroccan hospitality. It bridges high-precision medical science with the understated luxury of an exclusive private practice in Fès. The interface rejects cold, sterile clinical conventions in favor of warmth, surgical clarity, and bespoke reassurance.

### Aesthetic Paradigm: Medical Luxury Minimalism
- **Surgical Precision:** Strict proportions, crisp baseline alignments, and ample breathing room that instill immediate confidence.
- **Organic Warmth:** Architectural surfaces influenced by modern Moroccan interiors—warm limestone whites, filtered light, and delicate brushed brass touches.
- **Tactile Caliber:** Elements convey high-grade materials through gossamer borders, soft dual-tint shadows, and muted metallic focal points.

### Cultural & Linguistic Context
All typography and interaction patterns prioritize French typography rules (non-breaking spaces before colons, guillemets `« »`, formatted Moroccan phone patterns `+212 (0)5 XX XX XX XX`, and price units displayed in Dirhams `MAD`). Visual balance respects bilingual harmony where Arabic accents or script overlays may occur alongside French medical terminology.

## Colors

### Palette Architecture
The palette pairs the clinical authority of oceanic deep navy with restorative sea-glass teal and warm artisanal gold.

- **Primary (`#123047` - Bleu Profond):** The anchor of institutional trust and architectural permanence. Used for primary typography, structural frames, and primary command actions.
- **Secondary (`#73B8B5` - Sarcelle Douce):** A restorative, clinical-grade mineral tone evoking hygiene, renewal, and calm. Used for interactive states, key metrics, and progressive disclosures.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#C9AD80` - Or Brossé):** A sophisticated satin gold reflecting high-end dental prosthetics, prestige certifications, and refined navigational badges. Never used loudly; applied sparingly as hairline borders, badges, and precision indicators.
- **Neutral Primary (`#263238` - Anthracite Minéral):** High-legibility neutral for long-form medical text, consultation breakdowns, and diagnostic readouts.
- **Canvas Base (`#FAFBFA` - Blanc Calcaire):** A soft, eye-resting warm white that eliminates harsh screen glare.
- **Surface Elevation (`#F1F6F7` - Brume Clinique):** Tinted blue-gray for modular containers, interactive card backdrops, and input field states.

### Contrast & Functional Roles
- **Surface Contrast:** `#FAFBFA` canvas against `#F1F6F7` surface creates a subtle 1.08:1 surface distinction, preserved through hairline borders (`rgba(18, 48, 71, 0.08)`).
- **Text Readability:** Charcoal text (`#263238`) achieves an accessible contrast ratio of 11.2:1 against canvas and surfaces.
- **Medical Validation:**
  - *Success / Hygiène Confirmée:* `#2E7D32` with `#E8F5E9` background.
  - *Attention / Rendez-vous Requis:* `#C9AD80` with `rgba(201, 173, 128, 0.12)` background.
  - *Alerte Médicale:* `#B71C1C` with `#FFEBEE` background.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings
- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crafted with balanced geometric proportions and gentle curves. Conveys progressive dentistry, modern orthodontics, and architectural precision.
- **Section Headers & Metric Indicators (Manrope):** Clean, structural sans-serif used for card titles, clinical sub-categories, and tabbed navigation.
- **Narrative & Medical Data (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and meticulously spaced for patient treatment plans, medical histories, and consent forms.

### Stylistic Guidelines
- Set titles in sentence case (`Chirurgie guidée & implants`), never all-caps, to maintain clinical warmth.
- Reserve uppercase exclusively for micro-labels (`label-sm`), such as tooth charting keys, specialized laboratory stages, or ISO certifications.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
- **12-Column Responsive Grid (Desktop ≥ 1024px):** Fixed max-width of `1280px` centered within a fluid viewport canvas. Gutters standard at `1.5rem` (24px) with margins at `3rem` (48px).
- **8-Column Grid (Tablet 640px – 1023px):** Fluid layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer padding.
- **4-Column Grid (Mobile ≤ 639px):** Fluid layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins.

### Spacing Scale Application
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-gaps between status dots and badge typography.
- `space-sm` (8px): Icon-to-text inline spacing, list item micro-spacing.
- `space-md` (16px): Compact card padding, field interior vertical padding, stacked metadata rows.
- `space-lg` (24px): Standard card container padding, grid item gaps, input group spacing.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section breaks within panels, spacing around treatment timeline milestones.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Tonal Depth
Visual elevation avoids heavy drop shadows. Depth is achieved via layering surfaces and diffuse atmospheric light:

- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** `#FAFBFA` base background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** `#FFFFFF` or `#F1F6F7` bordered by a 1px ghost stroke: `border: 1px solid rgba(18, 48, 71, 0.07)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover):** Subtle dual-layer illumination tinted by the navy base:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(18, 48, 71, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(18, 48, 71, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Treatment Sheets):** Focused overlay elevation:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 32px -8px rgba(18, 48, 71, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(18, 48, 71, 0.04)`.
  Complemented by a frosted medical backdrop blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background-color: rgba(250, 251, 250, 0.8)`.

### Border Accents & Refinement
Gold accents (`#C9AD80`) appear on surfaces as focused top hairline borders (`2px solid #C9AD80`) on selected cards (e.g., active prosthodontic plans or VIP patient rooms) rather than high-contrast shadows.

## Shapes

### Form Geometry
The geometric aesthetic relies on balanced `0.5rem` (8px) base corners with `1rem` (16px) for major card modules and `1.5rem` (24px) for elevated treatment panels.

- **Micro Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** `8px` (`rounded-md`). Balances ergonomic softness with architectural precision.
- **Cards, Modals & Diagnostic Sheets:** `16px` (`rounded-lg`). Creates approachable, non-intimidating containment for clinical content.
- **Feature Highlights & Booking Panels:** `24px` (`rounded-xl`). Used for flagship service presentations (e.g., *Prothèse Numérique 3D*).
- **Status Pills & Special Badges:** Full pill (`9999px`) reserved strictly for status chips (e.g., `En cours de fabrication`, `Confirmé`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Action Majeure):** Solid `#123047` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Height `48px`, horizontal padding `24px`, radius `8px`. Hover state subtly lightens to `#1D4463` with an accent underline or micro-shift. Focus ring: `2px solid #73B8B5` offset by `2px`.
- **Secondary (Action Clinique):** Transparent background with `1px solid rgba(18, 48, 71, 0.2)` border, text `#123047`. Hover transition fills to `#F1F6F7`.
- **Tertiary / Prestataire (Prestige Consultation):** Satin gold surface (`#C9AD80`) with `#123047` text for high-value conversions (*Prendre Rendez-vous Privé*).

### Input Fields & Selects
- Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(18, 48, 71, 0.12)`, radius `8px`.
- Active / Focus: Border transitions to `#73B8B5` with a diffuse ambient glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(115, 184, 181, 0.2)`).
- Input labels use `Manrope` (`label-lg`) positioned above the field with color `#123047`.
- Helper notes and medical error states sit `4px` below the field in `body-sm`.

### Cards & Modules
- **Service & Specialty Cards:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(18, 48, 71, 0.06)`, padding `24px`. Hover reveals a top highlight border in `#73B8B5` or `#C9AD80`.
- **Case Review / Before-After Cards:** Dual split containers with `#F1F6F7` structural surrounds, separating photographic documentation from technical notes (shade matching, material composition: *Zircone multicouche*).

### Chips & Status Badges
- Height `28px`, padding `0 12px`, radius `9999px`.
- **Teal Chip (Orthodontie / Hygiène):** Background `rgba(115, 184, 181, 0.15)`, text `#123047`, border `1px solid rgba(115, 184, 181, 0.3)`.
- **Gold Chip (Prothèse / Esthétique VIP):** Background `rgba(201, 173, 128, 0.15)`, text `#123047`, border `1px solid rgba(201, 173, 128, 0.4)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box dimensions `20x20px`, corner radius `4px` for checkboxes, circular for radios.
- Unchecked: `1.5px solid rgba(18, 48, 71, 0.25)` on `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: `#123047` background with crisp white checkmark / inner dot.

### Specialized Domain Component: Dental Charting Index (Schéma Dentaire)
- Interactive tooth quadrant schematic rendered in clean vector lines using `#123047` (normal state), `#73B8B5` (treated/planned), and `#C9AD80` (prosthesis installed).
- Tooth nodes feature interactive popovers showing tooth reference numbers (ISO/FDI two-digit system) along with clinical restoration history.
